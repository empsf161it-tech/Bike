Our Genesis

FROM RACE TELEMETRY TO SHOWROOM SUPREMACY

APEX MOTORS was established in 2008 by two former World Superbike race engineers who shared a single vision: bringing factory-level racing technology, tuning, and customer care to street riders and track enthusiasts alike.

Our 25,000 sq. ft. flagship facility houses an architectural showroom, a state-of-the-art Superflow dyno bay, a custom composite bodywork lab, and an executive VIP customer lounge.

Our Vision

To redefine luxury motorcycle ownership through extreme technical transparency and uncompromised safety standards.

Our Mission

Empower riders with high-performance machines, race-calibrated dyno tuning, and unforgettable track day experiences.

Journey & Growth

OUR HISTORICAL MILESTONES

Over 18 years of relentless pursuit of motorcycling perfection.

2008

Founded in Motor City

Started as a boutique 2-bay race tuning shop catering exclusively to amateur road racers.

2014

Official Dealership License

Secured official distribution rights for flagship European & Japanese motorcycle manufacturers.

2019

Superflow Dyno Lab Launch

Installed our custom All-Wheel Superflow Dyno test cell with climate & humidity simulation controls.

2023

Track Rental Academy

Launched our turn-key Track Day rental fleet and VIP 1-on-1 race coaching program.

2026

Electric Hyperbike Division

Unveiled our next-gen electric hyperbike customization lab and fast-charge network partnership.
