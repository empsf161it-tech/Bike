ANALYZING THE 2026 DESMODROMIC V4 ENGINE MAPS ON THE DYNO

Marco Rossi Sept 02, 2026 6 min read

When tuning high-revving 90° V4 motorcycle engines, small adjustments to ignition timing and air-fuel ratio (AFR) targets can yield substantial gains in mid-range torque while smoothing throttle connection on lean angles.

The Challenge of High-Compression V4 Architecture

Modern superbike engines operate at static compression ratios as high as 14.0:1. At 14,500 RPM, the mechanical stresses on titanium connecting rods and desmodromic valve actuations leave zero margin for detonation or erratic fuel delivery.

"In dyno testing, we saw a remarkable 8.4 horsepower gain at 13,200 RPM simply by smoothing the AFR transition between cylinder banks 1-2 and 3-4."

Dyno Calibration Methodology

Using our Superflow All-Wheel Motorcycle Dyno, we logged over 40 parameters per millisecond including exhaust gas temperatures (EGT), individual cylinder AFR via wideband O2 sensors, and intake manifold pressure.

Key Findings & Results

  • Peak Horsepower: Increased from 208.2 HP (stock) to 216.6 HP at the rear wheel with Akrapovič full titanium exhaust system.
  • Torque Curve: Eliminated the stock 6,000 RPM emissions dip, boosting mid-range grunt by +11.2 Nm.
  • Throttle Response: Reduced throttle lag during quick-on/off transitions by recalibrating ride-by-wire servo speed maps.
